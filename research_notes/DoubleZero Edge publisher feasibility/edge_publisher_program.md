# DoubleZero Edge and the third-party publisher ("Edge for Builders" / RFC-28 builder stake) program, as of 16 October 2026

Scope note: these notes cover what a third-party market-data vendor (e.g. BlockSize Capital, 110-exchange aggregation, cross-venue VWAP and top-of-book) must do, have and pay to publish on DoubleZero Edge, and the tooling DoubleZero provides. Everything marked "verified" has a URL; everything else is labelled inference or gap. Dates are given wherever the source carries one. Research was done 16 Oct 2026 with web search, direct page downloads (doublezero.xyz blocks generic fetchers with HTTP 403 but serves a browser user agent), the GitHub API/raw files, the crates.io API, the CoinGecko API, the DeFiLlama API and DoubleZero's own data API.

## Key question 1: Does an official "Edge for Builders" announcement or docs page exist, and what is the program's public status?

### Takeaway
No public page, blog post, press release or indexed X post using the phrase "Edge for Builders" could be found as of 16 Oct 2026. The mechanism itself exists and is partly shipped in public code under the name "RFC-28" / "builder stake" / "edge builder feed" (Sept-Oct 2026 releases), but the RFC text is not public, the on-chain feature flag that enables staked feeds was "on no cluster" as of 11 Sep 2026, and a DoubleZero engineer wrote on 30 Sep 2026 that "no builder payout exists yet". Treat the program as pre-launch / private-preview; the public-facing route today is the "Are you a venue or exchange? Distribute your market data over DoubleZero" contact path on the Edge page and "Multicast as a Service".

### Cited Findings
- The Edge product page carries a "Multicast as a Service — Run a private feed. A private multicast group on DoubleZero's network. Full control over who publishes, who subscribes, and who gets access. Are you a venue or exchange? Distribute your market data over DoubleZero. One connection from your infrastructure, delivered to every subscriber simultaneously." block, but no "builders" page; candidate URLs /edge/publish, /builders, /edge/builders, /dz-edge-builders all return 404 — [DoubleZero Edge page](https://doublezero.xyz/dz-edge) (downloaded 16 Oct 2026).
- The Multicast-as-a-Service page says: "Publishers and subscribers are defined by the firm using the network ... Pricing is based on publishers, subscribers, and overall bandwidth — reach out to discuss pricing for a specific use case"; publisher "connects via GRE/BGP to the nearest DoubleZero Device", network "replicates in hardware, transports over dedicated fiber", subscribers "receive the feed as standard UDP multicast"; it lists "Exchanges and Trading Venues", "Data Providers and Oracle Networks" and "Trading Firms: Internal Distribution" as target users — [doublezero.xyz/multicast](https://doublezero.xyz/multicast).
- The journal index (16 Oct 2026) lists only these Edge-related posts: Binance (8 Oct), Phoenix (29 Sep), Hyperliquid launch PR and "Uncapped" essay (24 Sep), Kalshi elections expansion, Q2 2026 token-holder update (18 Aug), Kalshi launch (Aug), Edge introduction (11 Mar) — none about a builder program — [DoubleZero Journal](https://doublezero.xyz/journal).
- GitHub PR #4383 "serviceability: add the edge builder feed chain" (opened and closed 30 Sep 2026, author martinsander00, resolves private issue malbeclabs/infra#2828) proposed `FeedChain::EdgeBuilder = 3` "for a feed that an edge builder deployed. The feed program reads this value to pay the month's rewards to the builder instead of to validators" and CLI `doublezero feed create --chain edge-builder`. Reviewer bgm-malbeclabs noted "the feed program does not read the ledger; an offchain process copies the value, and no builder payout exists yet" and that the existing `builder` field already marks builder feeds. The PR was closed; follow-up is in the private repo malbeclabs/doublezero-shreds#796. The PR's test created "a Polymarket feed with EdgeBuilder" — [PR #4383](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/pull/4383).
- Release v0.44.0 (2 Oct 2026): "`CreateFeed` refuses a feed with a builder and a chain ... The rewards process uses either the builder or the chain to find who gets the rewards. (#4385)" — [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- Release v0.40.0 (11 Sep 2026): "`Feed` carries the RFC-28 stake terms: `builder`, `stake_ref`, `spec_id`, `sla_hash`, `committed_rate_bits_per_sec` and a lifecycle `status`. Setting them needs the new `allow-staked-feeds` feature flag, which no cluster has, so `CreateFeed` refuses a builder until the stake mirror and the attestor exist" — [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- The public `rfcs/` directory of malbeclabs/doublezero contains rfc0-rfc28, but its "RFC-28" is "Declarative Access Pass Reconciliation" (status Draft), not the builder-bond RFC; the bond RFC-28 quoted in code comments is therefore an internal document — [rfcs/rfc28-declarative-access-passes.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/rfcs/rfc28-declarative-access-passes.md).
- The Edge wire-format spec is explicitly written for third-party venues: "Domain-agnostic. Anything with a two-sided book — bids and asks at prices with quantities — is a valid instrument: crypto spot, equities, futures, FX, prediction markets, or anything else" and "Source-attributed ... with many [publishers] it is essential" — [top-of-book/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/top-of-book/spec.md).
- The publisher guide in edge-multicast-ref opens "Bringing up a feed — From nothing to a feed that publishes, records and can be audited afterwards" and distinguishes "A venue on an existing feed" (reuse `dz-edge-tob`/`-mbp`/`-refdata`, work lands "in the venue's repository, mostly") from "A new feed type" — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md).
- Edge was announced 11 Mar 2026 ("DoubleZero Introduces 'Edge'", Eden Vander Zee) with Solana shreds as first feed and "additional data feeds to follow"; the public beta launch was reported 16 Apr 2026 — [DoubleZero journal](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/doublezero-introduces-edge-a-new-real-time-market-data-platform); [The Block, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/397715/doublezero-rolls-out-edge-a-low-latency-onchain-data-delivery-service-on-its-global-fiber-depin-infrastructure).

### Inferences
- The "Edge for Builders" name heard on a call maps to what the code calls RFC-28 "builders" (a party that posts a 2Z bond, deploys a feed, and is paid the publisher share). The pieces visible in public code (bond program, stake mirror, feed lifecycle, builder-keyed rewards) were landed between 11 Sep and 2 Oct 2026, consistent with an "early October 2026" announcement to selected counterparties rather than a public launch.
- Every venue currently live on Edge (Kalshi, Hyperliquid, Phoenix, Binance) was brought up by DoubleZero/Malbec Labs or validator partners, not by a self-serve third-party builder; a vendor like BlockSize would be among the first external builders.

### Gaps
- No public announcement text, application form, or docs page for "Edge for Builders" was found; the RFC-28 document itself is not public. Terms quoted here come from code comments and the changelog.
- Whether the program is open to data aggregators/derived-data vendors (VWAP, composite top-of-book) versus only to venues is not stated anywhere public. The Source ID registry says a Source ID "names a matching engine, not a venue", which does not obviously accommodate a composite/derived cross-venue instrument (see Key question 8).

## Key question 2: What is the publisher onboarding flow (application, bond, multicast group, hardware/connection, host location)?

### Takeaway
Public evidence shows a two-layer flow: (a) on-chain, a builder posts a 2Z bond into the `builder-stake` program on Solana, a relayer mirrors it to the DZ ledger, the builder creates a `Feed` (one per metro) referencing an `edge-feed-spec` spec id, an SLA hash and a committed bit rate, the feed starts `Pending` and an attestor's conformance verdict moves it to `Active`, after which seats sell; (b) off-chain, the publisher implements a Rust adapter against the `edge-multicast-ref` crates, is allocated one multicast group per feed and one UDP port per port role by DoubleZero's infrastructure team, and runs the publisher binary on any IP-connected host that connects to the nearest DoubleZero Device over GRE/BGP with `doublezero connect multicast --publish <group>`. No published hardware spec, hosting requirement or application form exists.

### Cited Findings
- Feed lifecycle in code: "A feed created without a stake is `Active` on creation ... A staked feed starts `Pending` and an attestor verdict moves it to `Active`." States: Pending (0, "Staked and created, waiting on a conformance verdict. No seats sell."), Active (1, "Publishing and sellable."), Halted (2, "Publication stopped by the builder. Resumable."), Retired (3), Retiring (4, "with the notice to seat holders running") — [state/feed.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/feed.rs).
- `Feed` account fields: `code`, `name`, `exchange` (metro, PDA seed; "One `feed_key` is one feed in one metro (e.g. `shreds@tokyo`)"), `groups` (multicast groups), `builder` ("The builder that deployed this feed and posted its bond"), `stake_ref` (BuilderStake PDA on Solana), `spec_id` ("The `edge-feed-spec` wire format this feed conforms to, as `<spec>@<version>` (e.g. `top-of-book@v1.0.0`)"), `sla_hash` ("SHA-256 of the service level the builder declared at deployment. The declaration lives offchain; slashing measures against it"), `committed_rate_bits_per_sec` ("`u64::MAX` is the unmetered tier"), `status`, `halted_by` ("A halt by an operator is not the builder's to lift"), `retires_at`, `feed_chain` — [state/feed.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/feed.rs).
- End-to-end sequence tested in v0.41.0 (16 Sep 2026): "a relayer writes a `StakeMirror`, a builder holding no permission creates a feed against it, grants its own publisher the right to send, and an operator activates it." Also: "A covering stake authorizes `CreateFeed` on its own, so a builder holding no permission deploys a feed against the bond it posted." And "A feed's multicast groups are created with `owner` set to the builder, and `AddMulticastGroupPubAllowlist` authorizes on `mgroup.owner == payer`, so the builder grants its own publish rights without the catalog admin" — [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- Retirement: "Retiring starts a thirty-day notice and is terminal; a retiring feed keeps serving the seat holders it has and takes no new ones. Finalizing is permissionless once the notice elapses." (v0.40.0) — [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- CLI feed verbs: `doublezero feed create --code <code> --name <name> --exchange <metro> --group <mgroup>... [--chain solana|hyperliquid]`, plus `feed update|list|delete|activate|halt|resume|retire|finalize-retirement`; `feed list --code shreds --exchange xlax` — [cli/src/feed/create.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/cli/src/feed/create.rs); [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- Off-chain publisher steps (venue on an existing feed): 1. implement one type implementing `dz_adapter_core::Adapter` (methods `message_types`, `poll_listings`, `on_connected`, `poll_upstream`, `on_payload`, `on_disconnected`, `snapshot`) in the venue's own repository; 2. depend on the crates by git tag (`dz-adapter-core`, `dz-publisher-runtime`, `dz-ingress-core` with `websocket`, or `dz-ingress-fix`) — "A tag in this repository is the release; there is nothing on crates.io"; 3. write a binary that is "a registry and nothing else" calling `dz_publisher_runtime::run(AdapterRegistry::new().with("a-venue-tob", ...))`; 4. prove offline with `[adapter.replay]`; 5. one TOML configuration with `venue`, `[egress] pin = "<source ip>"`, `ttl` (required, no default), `[[feed]] spec = "top-of-book"`, `channel_id`, `source_id`, `multicast_group`, ports — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md).
- Ingress transports available to a publisher adapter: `websocket`, `fix` ("TLS is on with no key anywhere to turn it off"), `poll` (REST catalogue); `multicast`, `filetail`, `uds` are named but "have no crate behind them yet" — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md).
- Multicast group and port allocation is done by DoubleZero's infrastructure, not by the builder: "This repository holds no addresses, no hosts and no inventory ... 1. Allocate the multicast group and ports. One group per feed, one port per port role, from the range your fleet owns. Two feeds sharing a group is the [mistake] ... 2. Decide the publisher's egress interface and its source address" — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md, "The infrastructure repositories"](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md).
- Multi-host publishing: `id_allocation = "derived"` "is for a channel published from more than one host" (instrument IDs derived by CRC-32 of a key so hosts agree without coordination); multiple publishers of the same feed is the production pattern for Hyperliquid ("Ten separate sources are live today ... each publishing on its own set of channels ... First arrival wins") and Phoenix ("Multiple publishers send each feed on the same groups, channel, and ports") — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md); [Uncapped: Hyperliquid on Edge, 24 Sep 2026](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/hyperliquid-market-data-on-doublezero-edge); [docs: Phoenix](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/phoenix/).
- Network connection for any publisher: "Publisher connects via GRE/BGP to the nearest DoubleZero Device"; "Publisher: Any IP-connected Server"; generic client command `doublezero connect multicast --publish <feed> [--subscribe <feed>...]`; the client reports "DoubleZero ID, your public IP, the device selected, and 'User Provisioned'"; `doublezero user list --client-ip <ip>` shows user type "Multicast" and the access pass — [doublezero.xyz/multicast](https://doublezero.xyz/multicast); [docs: Other multicast](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/reference/other-multicast/).
- Validator (shreds) publisher path for comparison: `doublezero connect ibrl && doublezero connect multicast --publish edge-solana-shreds` after adding `--shred-receiver-address 233.84.178.1:7733`; verification on the publisher-check dashboard; "Connecting to DoubleZero implies acceptance of the DoubleZero Terms of Service" — [docs: Publish shreds](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/solana/edge/publish/).
- Existing venue feeds' data ingestion sits next to the venue: Hyperliquid "sources data directly from HyperCore validators via sentry node connections" with publishers "in Tokyo"; Binance data "ingested in Tokyo"; Phoenix books are decoded by DoubleZero from Edge's own shreds — [doublezero.xyz/hyperliquid](https://doublezero.xyz/hyperliquid); [Binance journal, 8 Oct 2026](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/doublezero-adds-binance-market-data-to-edge); [Phoenix PR, 29 Sep 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/doublezero-adds-phoenix-perpetual-futures-to-multi-venue-market-data-platform-edge-302892440.html).
- Conformance tooling exists for the attestation step: `dz-conformance` "Conformance subscriber for publishers: validates one feed — live multicast or capture replay (pcap or pcapng) — against an explicit rule catalog drawn from these specs, and returns a CI-friendly exit code"; it covers TOB, Midpoint, MBO, MBP and Reference Data; "Order-Intent and Perp Stats feeds have no checker yet" — [edge-feed-spec README](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec).
- The DoubleZero network had 31 metros, 85 enabled Edge devices (99 total) on 16 Oct 2026 — [data API: edge/shreds/overview](https://data.doublezero.xyz/api/v1/edge/shreds/overview); and "31+ global metros, 90+ active devices, 8.74 Tbps" — [doublezero.xyz/hyperliquid](https://doublezero.xyz/hyperliquid).

### Inferences
- A BlockSize publisher host would need: a Linux server with a public IP in (or near) a DoubleZero metro, the `doublezero` apt client, GRE/BGP (TCP 179), PIM, firewall rules, and the Rust publisher binary; the Solana keypair used as DoubleZero ID is the identity that posts the bond and owns the feed. Latency to the chosen DoubleZero Device (checked with `doublezero latency`) matters more than a specific data centre.
- Because `Feed` is scoped to one metro, a vendor wanting to sell in Frankfurt, New York and Tokyo appears to need one Feed account per metro (each with its own groups); whether each needs its own bond is unclear (code says "one bond backs one feed" and the `StakeMirror` is keyed per stake).
- The "attestor" conformance verdict will likely be run with `dz-conformance` against the live multicast or a pcap; publishers should run it in CI before applying.

### Gaps
- No public application form, SLA template (`sla_hash` input), hardware spec, bandwidth minimums, or list of allowed publisher locations. The Edge interest form (doublezero.xyz/edge-form) is aimed at subscribers.
- Who the "attestor" and the "relayer / STAKE_ORACLE" are (DoubleZero Foundation? Malbec Labs?) is not stated publicly.
- Whether a publisher needs an access pass / pays network fees for its own publish connection is not documented; the Multicast-as-a-Service page says pricing depends on "publishers, subscribers, and overall bandwidth" and is negotiated.

## Key question 3: 2Z bond mechanics (sizing vs committed data rate, who posts it, hold, slashing/forfeiture)

### Takeaway
The bond is posted by the builder into the `builder-stake` Solana program (`dzbschFChpPoWihZFdnYjyzHJicZwPHb6QTntHjhLki`) in 2Z, sized by a three-tier table keyed to the feed's committed bit rate (<=1 Gbps, <=5 Gbps, unmetered), which RFC-28 quotes as "about $100k, $200k and $500k" but fixes in 2Z "at the price prevailing when the tier is set"; one bond collateralises exactly one feed; a 180-day minimum hold runs from the first bond; excess above the requirement is withdrawable after the hold; the bond is "forfeitable by slashing" measured against the declared SLA, but slashing is not yet implemented and the tier table is zero (program paused) until an admin sets it.

### Cited Findings
- Program and purpose: "Custody for the 2Z bond a builder posts before deploying a feed, per RFC-28. This program holds user bonds and will hold burn authority over them once slashing exists." `declare_id!("dzbschFChpPoWihZFdnYjyzHJicZwPHb6QTntHjhLki")`; 2Z mint decimals 8 — [builder-stake lib.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/lib.rs).
- Tier table: "RFC-28 quotes the tiers in dollars (about $100k, $200k and $500k) but fixes the bond 'in 2Z, at the price prevailing when the tier is set'. So these are 2Z amounts an admin sets, not a price feed this program reads." Rate ceilings compiled in: `UP_TO_1GBPS_BITS_PER_SEC = 1_000_000_000`, `UP_TO_5GBPS_BITS_PER_SEC = 5_000_000_000`; above 5 Gbps is unmetered. "A zero requirement would let a builder deploy a feed against nothing" so an unset table sizes nothing; table must be monotonically non-decreasing — [tier_parameters.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/state/tier_parameters.rs).
- Stake tiers on the DZ ledger: `StakeTier { None=0, UpTo1Gbps=1, UpTo5Gbps=2, Unmetered=3 }`; "the tier is what a stake buys: the highest rate a feed backed by it may commit to"; "A zero rate is not a commitment, so no tier covers it" — [state/stake_mirror.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/stake_mirror.rs).
- One bond per feed: "RFC-28 collateralizes each feed on its own bond: 'A builder running a second feed posts a second bond. Each feed is collateralized on its own, so slashing one never reaches another.'" `BuilderStake` PDA seeded by builder + `stake_index`; "a builder holds as many of these as it runs feeds" — [builder_stake.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/state/builder_stake.rs).
- Hold and withdrawal: "RFC-28's six-month minimum hold, as 180 days" (`HOLD_SECONDS = 180*24*60*60`); "What the builder may take back right now: everything above the requirement, and nothing at all until the hold elapses"; "The first bond starts the hold. Later ones do not restart it: a repricing can force a top-up, and that must not push the builder's withdrawal date out"; `required_2z_amount` "Follows the tier table while the stake is short, and stops moving once the stake is funded" — [builder_stake.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/state/builder_stake.rs); [processor.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/processor.rs).
- Instructions: `InitializeProgram`, `SetAdmin`, `ConfigureProgram`, `InitializeBuilderStake`, `PostBond`, `Withdraw`, `SetHoldExpiry` ("a development-build instruction" for devnet demos). "A bond rather than a deposit: it is returnable after the hold and forfeitable by slashing" (v0.40.0, 11 Sep 2026) — [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md); [processor.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/processor.rs).
- Program starts paused: "What a bond costs at each rate tier. Zero until an admin sets it, which is why a fresh deployment starts paused: it can size no bond." — [program_config.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/solana/programs/builder-stake/src/state/program_config.rs).
- Mirror to the DZ ledger: "A DZ ledger program cannot read a Solana account, so a relayer watches `builder-stake` on Solana and writes what it saw here. This is an assertion, not a proof ... who is allowed to sign one is a trust decision"; `WriteStakeMirror` (variant 119) requires the `STAKE_ORACLE` permission; "`CreateFeed` refuses a staked feed whose committed rate the stake tier does not cover ... and refuses a stake that already backs a feed" — [state/stake_mirror.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/stake_mirror.rs); [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- Slashing basis: `sla_hash` is the "SHA-256 of the service level the builder declared at deployment. The declaration lives offchain; slashing measures against it, so the hash pins which text was declared" — [state/feed.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/feed.rs).
- The protocol-level rationale for burns/bonds: the 10% burn exists "to prevent self-dealing attacks wherein network contributors send inorganic traffic over their own links" — [Q2 2026 Token Holder Update, 18 Aug 2026](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/doublezero-q2-2026-token-holder-update).
- 2Z market on 16 Oct 2026 (CoinGecko API, 19:12 UTC): price $0.0323, market cap $167.3M (rank 201), FDV $322.6M, circulating 5.18B of 10B max, ATH $0.894 (2 Oct 2025), ATL $0.0315 (15 Oct 2026) — [CoinGecko markets API](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/markets?vs_currency=usd&ids=doublezero).

### Inferences
- At $0.0323/2Z the dollar tiers translate to roughly 3.1M 2Z (<=1 Gbps), 6.2M 2Z (<=5 Gbps) and 15.5M 2Z (unmetered); but because the admin fixes the 2Z amount when the tier is set, the live number could differ materially from this, and a 2Z rally after setting would raise the dollar value locked. A top-of-book + VWAP feed for 110 exchanges is very likely far below 1 Gbps (Hyperliquid's reference channel is 4-5 kbit/s; a TOB quote is 60 bytes), so the lowest tier (about $100k) is the relevant one unless DoubleZero also offers a smaller tier not visible in code.
- "Forfeitable by slashing" is design intent; no slashing instruction exists in the public program ("will hold burn authority over them once slashing exists"), so near-term risk to a bond is governance/manual, not automatic.
- The bond is capital locked for at least 6 months, not a fee; it is returnable (excess immediately after hold, principal presumably once the feed retires — the code only shows excess-above-requirement being withdrawable, so principal return after retirement is not evidenced).

### Gaps
- The exact 2Z amounts per tier have not been set publicly (program config is zero/paused until configured; no mainnet config was found). Whether the tier table has already been configured on mainnet-beta is unknown.
- How principal is returned when a feed retires (does `required_2z_amount` drop to zero?) is not visible in public code.
- Who can slash, by what measurement against the SLA, and in what proportion, is not public.
- A smaller/cheaper tier for low-rate feeds (kbit/s class) is not in code; a vendor should ask.

## Key question 4: Revenue split (10% burn, then 50/50 publisher vs network contributors?) — verification

### Takeaway
Verified: all subscription revenue (USDC/USD/card/ACH) is converted to 2Z; 10% is burned; the remaining 90% is split 50% to network (fiber) contributors and 50% to "data originators"/publishers. For the Solana shreds feed the publisher half is further split 32.5% validators / 17.5% validator-client teams (of the 90%). Federa's 26 Sep 2026 summary states it as 45% fiber providers, 45% data originators, 10% burn of gross, which is the same arithmetic. Kalshi's press release says Edge publishers "typically get a percentage share of the post-burn subscription fees"; the builder-feed code pays "the month's rewards to the builder instead of to validators", so the 50%-of-post-burn publisher share applies to builders by inference, not by an explicit public statement.

### Cited Findings
- "The Edge revenue model allows validators to earn a proportional share of subscriber fees. After a 10% burn, revenue will be programmatically split amongst validators producing shreds (e.g., 32.5% proportional to their shred distributions), validator client development teams (e.g., 17.5% proportional to their shred distributions), and DoubleZero network contributors (50%)." (11 Mar 2026) — [Edge introduction](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/doublezero-introduces-edge-a-new-real-time-market-data-platform).
- Validators page: "First, 10% of revenue in 2Z is burned for protocol security. The remaining 90% is distributed as follows: 50% DoubleZero network contributors, proportional to contribution; 32.5% Shred-originating validators, proportional to leader shreds; 17.5% Protocol client teams, proportional to shred distribution." Payments "distributed in 2Z ten epochs after fees are earned (approximately 20 days). To receive SOL or USDC instead, a validator can specify a destination address" — [doublezero.xyz/dz-edge/validators](https://doublezero.xyz/dz-edge/validators).
- Q2 2026 update: "Across the quarter, 10% of the 2Z fees were burned. Each of the validator (including client) and contributor share of the reward pool ran at 50% of the post-burn amount." Q2 revenue "approximately $330,000 in USDC across 39 subscription epochs, converting to roughly 4.35MM 2Z. Of that, 435,000 2Z was burned"; "Since launch, the protocol has burned approximately 1,800,000 2Z" — [Q2 2026 Token Holder Update](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/doublezero-q2-2026-token-holder-update).
- Austin Federa, X, 26 Sep 2026: "Fees are paid in stablecoin (or credit cards or ACH) and that swapped into [2Z] - 45% of that 2z is distributed to the fiber network providers ... - 45% of that is distributed to the data originators, which in this case are participating Solana validators and Hyperliquid validators. These income streams are segmented by network ... - 10% of the [2Z] is burned ... The protocol has no inflation." — [x.com/Austin_Federa/status/2103898130202644799](https://x.com/Austin_Federa/status/2103898130202644799).

## Key question 8: Edge wire protocol (transport, framing, sequencing, recovery, encoding, limits)

### Takeaway
Edge feeds are GRE-encapsulated UDP multicast (one 24-byte frame header per datagram, max 1,232 bytes), little-endian fixed-size binary messages with a 4-byte message header, schema-versioned (byte 3 for current TOB/MBO/MBP/order-intent/perp-stats, 1 for midpoint), per-channel u64 sequence numbers plus a reset counter, a two-port model (market data + reference data) with continuous in-band instrument-definition retransmission and, for depth feeds, in-band snapshot cycles on a third port; no retransmission/repair exists. It is closer in spirit to CME MDP 3.0 / Nasdaq ITCH than to SBE (no schema XML, fixed layouts, no repeating groups). Bandwidth tiers for publishers are the bond tiers (<=1 Gbps, <=5 Gbps, unmetered); no per-message rate limits are documented.

### Cited Findings
- Design principles: "Little-endian ... Fixed-size messages. Every message type has a constant length. No variable-length fields, no repeating groups ... Schema-versioned. The frame header carries a version byte. New fields append to messages; old decoders ignore trailing bytes ... Multicast-native. UDP multicast delivery. One frame per UDP datagram ... Instrument-ID based. Numeric u32 IDs on the market data path ... Source-attributed. Every price message carries a u16 Source ID ... Domain-agnostic." — [top-of-book/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/top-of-book/spec.md).
- Framing: "The maximum frame size is 1,232 bytes to leave room for GRE encapsulation headers used by the DoubleZero network's last-mile delivery." 24-byte frame header: Magic u16 `0x445A` ("DZ"; `0x4442` for MBP), Schema Version u8, Channel ID u8, Sequence Number u64 ("Monotonically increasing per channel, starting from 0. Resets to 0 when Reset Count changes"), Send Timestamp ns u64, Message Count u8, Reset Count u8, Frame Length u16. 4-byte application header: Type u8, Length u8 (max 255), Flags u16 (bit 0 snapshot vs incremental). Instrument key is `(channel_id, instrument_id)` — [top-of-book/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/top-of-book/spec.md).
- TOB message set (v3.1.0): 0x01 Heartbeat 16B, 0x02 InstrumentDefinition 130B (refdata; Symbol char[64], Leg1/Leg2 char[8], Asset Class {1 Crypto Spot, 2 Prediction Binary, 3 Prediction Scalar, 4 Prediction Categorical, 5 Perpetual Future}, Price Exponent i8, Qty Exponent i8, Market Model {1 CLOB, 2 AMM}, Tick Size, Lot Size, Contract Value, Expiry, Settle Type, Price Bound, Manifest Seq), 0x03 Quote 60B (Instrument ID, Source ID, Update Flags bid/ask updated/gone, Source Timestamp, Bid/Ask Price i64 and Qty u64 with implied exponents, Bid/Ask Source Count), 0x04 Trade 52B (Aggressor Side, Trade Flags block/sweep/cross, Trade ID u64, Cumulative Volume), 0x06 EndOfSession 12B, 0x07 ManifestSummary 24B, 0x08 Liquidation 48B, 0x09 StrikeInterval 40B — [top-of-book/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/top-of-book/spec.md).
- Two-port model and recovery: "one multicast group with two destination ports": mktdata (Quote, Trade, Heartbeat, EndOfSession) and refdata (InstrumentDefinition, ManifestSummary, StrikeInterval); "A subscriber bootstrapping from a cold start MUST bind both ports"; ManifestSummary carries Manifest Seq + Instrument Count so a subscriber can detect a complete view; "Why Two Ports, Not Two Multicast Groups": refdata traffic is small — [reference-data/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/reference-data/spec.md). Operational cadence: InstrumentDefinition repeated at least every 30 s, ManifestSummary at least every 1 s (5 s on Binance Spot); "No retransmission. Delivery is fire-and-forget UDP. No market data repair"; lost TOB quotes "stay stale until that instrument's best bid or ask changes"; MBP/MBO recover via the snapshot port ("A cold start on MBP must bind the snapshot port"); any Reset Count change means "discard that channel's state" — [docs: Binance](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/binance/); [docs: Kalshi](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/kalshi/).
- Sequencing across publishers: "Multiple publishers send each feed on the same groups, channel, and ports. Key all state on source IP plus Channel ID"; gap detection "per source IP, channel ID, and destination port"; trade dedup on `(Source ID, instrument ID, trade ID)`; "There's no publisher ID field anywhere in [the format], and deduplication keys off the event's own content ... First arrival wins" — [docs: Phoenix](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/phoenix/); [Uncapped essay](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/hyperliquid-market-data-on-doublezero-edge).
- Port convention examples: Kalshi "Ports fall in the 30000-59999 band. The leading digit is the traffic class (3 = market, 4 = reference, 5 = snapshot), and the second digit identifies the feed ... The port is a socket filter applied on your host, because the fabric does not inspect UDP ports"; Hyperliquid "Reference port = market port + 1. Snapshot port (MBO only) = market port + 2"; heartbeats on UDP 5765 — [docs: Kalshi](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/kalshi/); [docs: Hyperliquid (Edge)](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/hyperliquid/edge/).
- Bandwidth: Hyperliquid's "reference channel rides alongside at four or five kilobits per second ... retransmitting about once a minute"; "Uncapped data, with no per-stream limits or token metering" for subscribers — [Uncapped essay](https://doublezero.xyz/journal/hyperliquid-market-data-on-doublezero-edge); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/doublezero-hyperliquid-order-book-feeds/).
- Source ID registry (v1.6.0): "A Source ID identifies the matching engine whose activity the message describes ... A Source ID names a matching engine, not a venue"; ranges 1-1023 production, 1024-32767 reserved, 32768-65535 "Private / experimental. Publishers MAY use these for internal testing"; assigned: 1 Hyperliquid, 2 Phoenix, 3 Kalshi, 4 Setai Financials, 5 Setai Commodities, 6 Binance USD-M futures, 7 XYZ (HIP-3), 8 Binance Spot, 9 Binance Coin-M futures (unpublished); `Code` follows TradingView venue naming; "To request a new Source ID, open a pull request against this file ... Adds a row ... with the next unused ID"; IDs are never reused — [sources/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/sources/spec.md).
- Versioning: "Within a major line, changes are additive only ... Field layouts and semantics will not change without a MAJOR release and a Schema Version bump, which decoders MUST reject"; tags `<spec>/vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` — [edge-feed-spec README](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec).
- Encoding of the publisher side is fixed by the crates: "Everything a specification already decided — the Instrument ID, the Source ID, the Channel ID, the sequence numbers, the scaling, the Update Flags byte, the Action, the datagram and its 1,232-byte cap — belongs to the crates here and is not expressible through the boundary" — [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md).
- Multicast group addressing used today is in 233.84.178.0/24 (e.g. shreds 233.84.178.1 UDP 7733; regional retransmit groups .12/.13/.14; Kalshi .3/.4/.17/.20; Binance .23/.31; Phoenix .24/.25; Hyperliquid .27-.30); `doublezero multicast group list` shows live values — [docs: Publish shreds](https://docs.doublezero.xyz/solana/edge/publish/) and venue docs above.

### Inferences
- A cross-venue VWAP is not a native message type. Options within the spec: (a) publish a Midpoint-style derived price (the `midpoint/` feed carries "a single derived mid price per instrument, computed from a venue's order book"), (b) publish a TOB Quote whose "venue" is BlockSize's composite engine with a new Source ID — but the registry defines Source ID as a matching engine, so DoubleZero would have to agree to treat a composite index as an engine or extend the registry; (c) propose a new feed type (the "right-hand column" path: spec first, golden vectors, codec crate, Go parser — "costs weeks").
- A 110-exchange composite TOB feed with a few thousand instruments fits easily in the <=1 Gbps tier; refdata for thousands of instruments at 130 B each repeated every 30 s is also small.
- The comparison to "NASDAQ ITCH / CME MDP 3.0" heard on the call is apt: fixed binary, sequence-numbered UDP multicast, separate instrument-definition channel, in-band snapshot recovery, no TCP retransmission service (unlike CME's TCP replay or Nasdaq MoldUDP64 re-request).

### Gaps
- Maximum message rates per channel/publisher and any policing by the fabric are not documented.
- How a derived/composite instrument (VWAP across venues) would be represented, and whether a non-matching-engine publisher can obtain a Source ID, is unresolved publicly.

## Key question 10: Summary checklist for a third-party publisher (what to do, have, pay) and open items to ask DoubleZero

### Takeaway
To publish on Edge today a vendor must (1) get DoubleZero to allocate a Source ID (PR to `edge-feed-spec`), a multicast group and port set per metro, (2) build a Rust adapter + publisher binary on `edge-multicast-ref` v0.6.0 and pass `dz-conformance`, (3) run it on an IP-connected host tunnelled (GRE/BGP) to a DoubleZero Device in each metro it sells into, (4) post a 2Z bond per feed (tier about $100k for <=1 Gbps, 180-day hold, slashable against a declared SLA) once the `builder-stake` table is configured and `allow-staked-feeds` is enabled, and (5) accept 45% of gross subscription revenue (50% of post-burn), paid monthly in 2Z (USDC/SOL option likely), with DoubleZero/Malbec operating the catalogue, pricing page, Edge Connect registry and billing.

### Cited Findings
- All findings above; in particular [state/feed.rs](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/blob/main/smartcontract/programs/doublezero-serviceability/src/state/feed.rs), [builder-stake](https://github.com/malbeclabs/doublezero/tree/main/solana/programs/builder-stake), [BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-multicast-ref/blob/main/BRINGING-UP-A-FEED.md), [sources/spec.md](https://github.com/malbeclabs/edge-feed-spec/blob/main/sources/spec.md), [dz-edge/validators](https://doublezero.xyz/dz-edge/validators), [Federa X post](https://x.com/Austin_Federa/status/2103898130202644799).

### Inferences
- Capital and cost stack for BlockSize (inference): bond ~3M 2Z (~$100k at $0.032; could be set at a different 2Z figure) locked >=180 days; engineering of an adapter (WebSocket/FIX/poll ingress exists; a composite engine would publish from BlockSize's own aggregation service); 1-3 publisher hosts per metro; no evidence of a listing fee beyond the bond and possible negotiated network charges.
- Revenue sensitivity: at Phoenix/Binance-like pricing ($50-$100/month/seat) a feed needs on the order of 100+ seats to clear meaningful revenue after the 55% that goes to burn and contributors; at Hyperliquid-like pricing ($900-$1,500 TOB) far fewer.

### Gaps (questions to put to DoubleZero)
- Is "Edge for Builders" open to aggregators/derived-data vendors, and how is a composite/VWAP instrument represented (new Source ID as a "matching engine", Midpoint feed, or new feed type)?
- Current 2Z amounts per bond tier on mainnet-beta; is there a sub-1 Gbps/low-rate tier; is the bond per feed per metro; how is principal returned at retirement; who adjudicates slashing and against what SLA text?
- Who sets the seat price for a builder feed, and is there regional (Tokyo/Global) pricing; are additional-host surcharges (8%, cap 50%) standard?
- Publisher payout: token, cadence (monthly), conversion timing, minimums; is the 45/45/10 split contractual for builders?
- Whether a publisher host must sit in a DoubleZero metro and whether DoubleZero charges publisher-side bandwidth under Multicast-as-a-Service.
- Timeline for enabling `allow-staked-feeds`, the attestor process, and the Edge Connect feed-registry listing.
